Hi! I am a second-year undergraduate student at Zhejiang University (ZJU), majoring in Mechanical Engineering.

My research interests lie in robot perception, 3D vision, dexterous manipulation, and representation learning. I am particularly interested in how visual and physical representations can support generalizable robot manipulation in hand-object interaction settings. Currently, I am working on 3D vision-based dexterous hand grasping under the supervision of Prof. [Hao Chen](https://stan-haochen.github.io/). Besides robotics, I have a long-standing interest in mathematics and am currently exploring Lean 4 and formal verification.

Outside of academics, I have a passion for music, especially the genres of Math-Rock, Post-Rock, Midwest Emo and Fusion Jazz. I aspire to form a band in the future and create my own music. I also enjoy reading novels and watching films during my leisure time. Bi Gan and Nolan are my favorite directors. Welcome to follow my CC98 account [BinaryRock@CC98](https://www.cc98.org/user/id/778349)(ZJU network required) for more updates about me!

Here are my answers to the questions of Chu Kochen,
- What have you come to ZJU for?
  To learn, to explore, to live, and to love.
- What kind of person will you become after graduation?
  Perhaps a researcher, an engineer, or a musician—or a blend of all three. Regardless of the title, I strive to become a better version of myself. I leave this question open for the future me to answer.
